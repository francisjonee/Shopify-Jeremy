# SCA Product Requirements — Jeremy Vision

## Core Goal

Build Second Chance Authenticators into the trusted digital infrastructure for collectible eyewear. Authentication is the first event in a permanent record that follows the eyewear through ownership, resale, transfer, service, and status changes.

## Final Build Direction

SCA will now be built **from scratch** as a new implementation. The inaccessible prior Ownership Bridge will not be recovered.

The current VPS is temporary construction/staging infrastructure only. The application must be portable to a future permanent server without rebuilding the product, regenerating permanent QR codes, or rewriting ownership history.

## Required Pre-Sale State

Every pair sold by Second Chance Eyewear should already have:

1. Authentication completed
2. Condition grade recorded
3. Registry / provenance record created
4. Unique SCA Certification ID
5. Permanent QR identity created by SCA
6. Printed QR included with the physical eyewear before shipment
7. Ownership state = `UNCLAIMED`

## Final Shopify / QR / Claim Model

The Shopify Dev app does **not** make Shopify the QR/provenance system.

Canonical responsibilities:

### Shopify

- product/variant/SKU commerce data;
- inventory state;
- order/payment/sale events;
- original purchaser reference.

### SCA

- unique certified physical eyewear record;
- Certification ID;
- authentication and condition;
- permanent QR identity;
- claim state;
- registered ownership and ownership history;
- transfers;
- service history;
- lost/stolen and registry status;
- My Collection.

The QR is generated by the **SCA application**, not by each Shopify product. The Shopify app may later show SCA links/status/QR actions inside Shopify Admin for staff convenience, but SCA remains canonical.

A Shopify product/variant is not guaranteed to equal one physical pair. Each uniquely certified physical eyewear item must have its own SCA record and QR. If a Shopify listing has multiple physical units, SCA must map the exact sold physical pair to the sale.

## Second Chance Eyewear Buyer Flow

1. Staff authenticates and grades the physical pair in SCA.
2. SCA creates the Digital Provenance Record, Certification ID, and permanent QR identity.
3. Staff prints the QR and includes it with the eyewear.
4. Customer purchases the pair from Second Chance Eyewear through Shopify.
5. Shopify confirms the paid order and identifies the purchased product/variant/order line.
6. SCA links that sale to the exact physical certified eyewear item.
7. SCA changes the claim state to `SOLD_AWAITING_CLAIM` when eligibility is valid.
8. Customer receives the physical eyewear and scans the printed QR.
9. QR opens the SCA record on the SCA website/collector application.
10. SCA shows that the eyewear has already been authenticated and offers a Claim Ownership action when eligible.
11. Customer signs in or creates an independent SCA collector account.
12. SCA verifies claim eligibility against the sale and/or secure claim mechanism.
13. Ownership is appended to the provenance record.
14. The item becomes `REGISTERED` and appears in **My Collection**.

A Shopify purchase makes the buyer **eligible to claim**. It does not automatically become permanent registered ownership.

Ownership is not registered at checkout. This allows cancellations, refunds, returns, failed delivery, and other commerce reversals to be handled before permanent ownership is recorded.

An email containing the same QR/claim link may be added later as a convenience, but the physical QR + SCA login/claim flow is the canonical ownership registration path.

## Shopify App Role

The Shopify app exists to connect the live Second Chance Eyewear commerce system to SCA and may eventually provide staff-facing conveniences inside Shopify Admin, such as:

- linked SCA Certification ID;
- authentication/claim status;
- open SCA record;
- view/print QR;
- mapping between Shopify order line and physical SCA item.

The app is not the provenance database. It does not automatically make Shopify generate one permanent QR per physical item.

Initial least-privilege scopes remain:

```text
read_products
read_inventory
read_orders
read_customers
```

Write scopes are added only when an approved feature specifically requires Shopify mutation.

## External Eyewear Flow

If a collector owns eyewear not purchased from Second Chance Eyewear:

1. Collector submits the piece for paid SCA authentication.
2. SCA authenticates and grades the item.
3. A new SCA Certification ID and permanent provenance record are created.
4. The item is added to the collector’s account after the applicable authentication/claim workflow.

## Digital Provenance Record

Each eyewear record should support:

- Model
- Manufacturer / brand
- Year
- Country of origin
- Materials
- Original specifications
- Authentication record
- Condition grade
- Inspection images
- Certification status
- Current ownership status
- Ownership history
- Transfer history
- Service history
- Market information (future)

The preferred product name is **SCA Digital Provenance Record™**. “Digital Passport” may still be used as consumer-facing UI language.

## Permanent QR

The QR must never expire and must resolve to a Jeremy-controlled SCA domain. The QR identifies the physical eyewear record, not a temporary session, Shopify checkout, email message, server IP, or VPS hostname.

A scan may show different behavior depending on record state:

- `UNCLAIMED`
- `SOLD_AWAITING_CLAIM`
- `REGISTERED`
- `TRANSFER_PENDING`
- `LOST`
- `STOLEN`
- `CERTIFICATION_CHANGED`

The same permanent QR remains valid throughout the item’s lifetime.

Development/staging QR codes must not be treated or printed as lifetime production identifiers until the production Jeremy-controlled QR base URL is finalized.

## Ownership History

Ownership must be event-based and append history. Do not model ownership as a single mutable owner field that destroys prior provenance.

Example:

- Original retail sale
- Owner 1
- Owner 2
- Owner 3

The current owner is derived from the active ownership state, while historical events remain preserved.

## Transfer Ownership

The registered owner can initiate a transfer. The receiving collector must accept/claim the transfer. The previous owner remains in provenance history after transfer completes.

## Service History

Repairs, lens replacements, polishing, tune-ups, inspections, and future services should append service events to the record.

## Lost / Stolen Registry

A registered owner can report an item lost or stolen. Public QR verification should show a warning status without exposing private owner information.

## Insurance Report

Future feature: generate a PDF containing relevant authentication, condition, photos, certification, serial/specification, ownership, and supporting record information.

## Market Information

Future feature, explicitly not an appraisal system. Potential fields include:

- Original retail MSRP
- Average resale
- Highest verified sale
- Lowest verified sale
- Last verified sale

## Collector Dashboard

**My Collection** should eventually include:

- Authenticated pieces
- Brands owned
- Certificates
- Service history
- Transfer history
- Downloadable records
- Optional purchase/insured-value summaries

## Collector Profiles

Future feature: optional public/private collector profile with non-sensitive collection statistics. Do not expose private pricing or personal information by default.

## Privacy Principle

Public QR scans must never expose email, phone, address, credentials, or other private account information. Public ownership display should be privacy-controlled and may show only a status or abbreviated display name.

## Infrastructure Permanence Principle

The temporary build server is disposable. The SCA system must be recoverable from private source control, PostgreSQL backup, durable media/object storage, protected environment/secrets, and deployment documentation.

Moving to the permanent server must not require permanent QR regeneration, provenance-history rewriting, or customer re-registration.
